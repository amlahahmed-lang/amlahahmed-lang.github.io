Privacy Policy – SUPREME RADAR

Last Updated: October 14, 2026

SUPREME RADAR is a cryptocurrency market monitoring and signal-tracking service that creates and shares informational content on social media platforms.

1. Information We Process

When users authorize our application through TikTok, we may process account information and authorization tokens necessary to connect the account and publish content. We do not request TikTok account passwords.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to authenticate authorized accounts, manage content uploads, and support video publishing functionality.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to TikTok as necessary to provide authorized publishing services.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect authentication credentials and related information against unauthorized access.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only as necessary for the operation of authorized services. Users may revoke access through TikTok account settings and request deletion of information by contacting us.

6. Third-Party Services

Our service may interact with TikTok and other third-party platforms. Their respective privacy policies also apply.

7. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, visit our Telegram channel: SUPREME RADAR.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this policy when our service or applicable requirements change. Updates will be published on this page.
